I joined the [CAPSULE](https://team.inria.fr/capsule/) team in September 2025
as a PhD student, under the supervision of
[Pierre-Alain Fouque](https://www.di.ens.fr/~fouque/)
and [Yixin Shen](https://yixinshen.github.io/).

My research aims to deepen our understanding of the quantum security of
lattice-based cryptographic schemes.
